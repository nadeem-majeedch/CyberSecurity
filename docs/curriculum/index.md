## The shape of the program

| Level | Theme | Modules | Guided hours | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Foundations | 15 | ~52 | **Published** — 15 modules, 11 labs |
| 2 | Tooling and Lab Craft | 8 | ~87 | **Published in part** — 6 of 8 modules, 18 labs |
| 3 | Applications, Cryptography and Identity | 8 | ~100 | **Published in part** — 6 of 8 modules, 17 labs |
| 4 | Defensive Operations | 7 | ~110 | **Published** — 7 modules, 22 labs |
| 5 | Advanced Practice | 8 | ~130 | **Published in part** — 6 of 8 modules, 18 labs |

**Published:** 40 of the 46 modules in the catalogue and 86 labs, approximately 400 guided hours.
**Whole programme:** approximately 480 guided hours, plus practice and write-ups.

The unwritten modules are CYB-207, CYB-208 (Level 2), CYB-305, CYB-306 (Level 3), CYB-507 and
CYB-508 (Level 5). Their outcomes are defined in the roadmap; no page is published for them, so an
unwritten module never appears in the navigation as if it were available.

## Design principles you will notice

- **Defender-first.** Every offensive technique is paired with how it is detected, logged, or prevented.
- **Lab before completion.** A module ends with an artefact, not a checkbox.
- **Isolation by construction.** All practice runs in your own lab; see the
  [Safety Policy](../policies/safety-policy.md).
- **Evidence discipline.** Note-taking and reporting are assessed, because they are the job.
- **Stated exclusions.** Hardware, ICS/SCADA, mobile app security, and large-scale adversary emulation
  are not covered — named rather than quietly omitted.

## Status vocabulary used throughout

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Published** | Lessons, labs, and assessment are available and verified |
| **Published in part** | Some modules of a level are complete; the missing ones are named, never stubbed |
| **In preparation** | Being authored now; partial material may exist |
| **Planned** | Structure and outcomes defined; no content published |

Every level page states its status. Nothing in this course presents an outline as finished material.
