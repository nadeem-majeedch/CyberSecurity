# CYB-105 · Ethics, Authorization, Responsible Disclosure and Cyber Law

**Level 1** · 2.5 hours · **Prerequisites:** [CYB-104](../04-threat-modeling-attack-surfaces/index.md)

## Why this module exists

This is the module that decides whether the rest of the course makes you employable or prosecutable.

The technical skills you are learning are dual-use: the same command that tests your own lab can be a
criminal act one IP address away. The difference is **authorization**, documented in advance, with a
defined scope.

Students often assume intent matters more than permission. It does not. Courts and disciplinary panels
assess what you did and whether you were permitted to do it. "I was learning" is not a defence anywhere.

## Module outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Explain what authorization is, what it must contain, and why verbal permission is insufficient.
- Write a scope statement for your own lab, and recognise an inadequate one.
- Apply a responsible disclosure process, including timelines and escalation.
- Name the key legal provisions that apply to unauthorized access in Pakistan and comparable
  jurisdictions, with the caveat that this is orientation, not legal advice.
- Classify realistic scenarios as authorized or not, and justify the classification.
- State the course's own rules and sign the acknowledgement.
